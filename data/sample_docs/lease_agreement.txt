RESIDENTIAL LEASE AGREEMENT
Property: Hauptstraße 42, 10827 Berlin
Tenant: Max Mustermann
Landlord: Immobilien GmbH Berlin
Duration: 12 months, starting 1 January 2025

PET POLICY
Pets are not permitted in the apartment without prior written consent from the landlord.
Small caged animals (hamsters, fish) are allowed without special permission.
Dogs and cats require a signed pet addendum and an additional security deposit of one month's cold rent.
The tenant is fully liable for any damage caused by pets.

MAINTENANCE AND REPAIRS
The landlord is responsible for structural repairs, heating systems, and plumbing.
Tenants must report any damage or defects within 48 hours of discovery by contacting hausmeister@immobilien-berlin.de.
Minor repairs under €100 (lightbulbs, door handles) are the tenant's responsibility.
Do not attempt to repair gas appliances yourself; always contact a certified technician.

MAINTENANCE REQUEST PROCEDURE
1. Email hausmeister@immobilien-berlin.de with photos and a description.
2. Include your apartment number and a phone number for callbacks.
3. Emergency repairs (water leak, heating failure in winter) call: +49 30 555 0123 — available 24/7.
4. Non-emergency requests will be addressed within 5 business days.

NOISE AND QUIET HOURS
Quiet hours (Ruhezeiten) apply between 22:00 and 07:00 on weekdays.
On Sundays and public holidays quiet hours apply all day.
Loud music, power tools, and parties are prohibited during quiet hours.
Musical instruments may be played between 10:00 and 12:00 and 15:00 and 19:00 on weekdays.

TERMINATION AND NOTICE PERIOD
Either party must give three months' written notice to terminate the lease.
Notice must be received in writing (letter or email) before the third working day of the month.
Early termination by mutual agreement is possible if the tenant provides a suitable replacement tenant.

SUBLETTING
Subletting any part of the apartment is not permitted without written landlord approval.
Unauthorized subletting (including Airbnb) is grounds for immediate termination.

UTILITIES
Cold rent: €950/month — due on the 3rd of each month.
Operating costs (Nebenkosten): €180/month advance payment; annual reconciliation in March.
Electricity and internet are arranged directly by the tenant.

ALTERATIONS
No structural alterations, wall painting (non-white), or drilling without prior written consent.
Satellite dishes and external antennas are not permitted.
All alterations must be reversed upon move-out unless otherwise agreed in writing.
